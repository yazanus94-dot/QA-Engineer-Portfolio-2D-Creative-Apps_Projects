# BUG-003: Exported PNG has a transparent background instead of white

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | Medium |
| Priority | P2 |
| Component | Export |
| Environment | Sketchpad v1.0 with `?bugs=BUG-003`, Chrome (latest) and Firefox (latest) |
| Related test case | TC-EXPO-001 |
| Reproducibility | Always (4/4) |

## Steps to reproduce
1. Draw a black pen stroke on the canvas (white background on screen).
2. Click **Export PNG**.
3. Open `sketch.png` in an image viewer with a dark theme, or place it on a colored slide.

## Expected result
The image has a solid white background that matches what was seen on the canvas.

## Actual result
The background is transparent: the black stroke is nearly invisible on dark backgrounds, and the checkerboard shows through in editors.

## Impact
Exports do not match the canvas ("what you see is not what you get"); shared drawings look broken.

## Notes
Workaround: draw a white rectangle behind the artwork before exporting.
